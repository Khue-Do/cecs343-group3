#Contributing Rules

##1. Branches: username/file. Nobody commits to main directly.
##2. Commits: message under 50 characters, say what and why.
##3. Pull Request: description says what, why, and how to check.
##4. Review: one approval from a teammate, never yourself, with a line comment.
##5. Merging: the author merges after aprroval, then deletes the branch.
